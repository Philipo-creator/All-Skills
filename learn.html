Lesson Notes

What is typography?

Typography is the art of arranging text so it is easy to read and pleasant to look at. It covers the fonts you choose, their size, spacing and how they work together on the page.

Main font groups

  • Serif: fonts with small strokes on the letters, often used for books and formal text.
  • Sans-serif: clean fonts without those strokes, common on websites and apps.
  • Script: fonts that look handwritten, best used sparingly.
  • Display: bold, decorative fonts for headings and logos.

Pairing fonts

A simple rule is to use two fonts at most: one for headings and one for body text. Choose fonts that look clearly different, then keep your sizes and spacing consistent.
