Frequently Asked Questions

What is AllSkills?

AllSkills is a skill discovery and structured learning platform with step-by-step roadmaps for many different skills.

Do I need an account to explore skills?

No. You can browse the whole library and view every roadmap without signing up. An account is needed to save your progress.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free gives you the full library, every roadmap, introductory lessons and a dashboard. Pro adds complete learning paths, advanced lessons and extra features.

How can I suggest a new skill?

Use the form above and choose "Suggest a skill" as the topic.
